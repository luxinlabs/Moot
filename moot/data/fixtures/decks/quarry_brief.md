Company: Quarry Labs
One-liner: AI geology models that tell mining companies where to drill.

## Problem
Exploration drilling is the costliest step in finding new mineral deposits, and most holes find nothing.

## Solution
Quarry Labs combines public geological surveys with satellite imagery to rank drill targets before a rig moves.

## Market
Global mineral exploration spending is about $13B per year.

## Traction
Pre-revenue. Two design-partner pilots started in September; no paid customers yet.

## Business model
Per-project licensing for exploration teams.

## Team
Founded by two former mining geologists and one ML engineer. Team of 4.

## Ask
Raising a $1.5M pre-seed to finish the pilots and hire two engineers.
