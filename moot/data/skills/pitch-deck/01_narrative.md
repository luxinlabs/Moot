---
title: Narrative arc
summary: The order of a pitch and what each slide must make the audience believe.
---
- One idea per slide. The slide title is the takeaway written as a full assertion ("Agents can't tell who to trust"), not a label ("Problem").
- Default arc for a product pitch: title → problem → why now → solution → product (how it works) → market → traction/proof → business model → competition/moat → team → ask → closing.
- Drop slides the sources cannot support rather than padding them; merge thin slides.
- The problem slide is about the customer's pain, with a concrete example. The solution slide says what changes for that customer.
- Traction is proof: users, revenue, pilots, a working demo. If there is no traction, say what has been proven so far and what the next milestone is.
- The ask names what you want (amount, partners, pilots) and what it unlocks.
- Adapt emphasis to the audience: investors want market + proof + ask; customers want pain + product + outcome; judges want novelty + demo + clarity.
