---
title: Slide writing
summary: How to write slides people can read in five seconds.
---
- 3–5 bullets per slide, each under 12 words. Prefer fragments over sentences.
- Lead with the concrete: numbers, names, examples. Remove adjectives that do not carry information ("innovative", "seamless", "cutting-edge").
- A single strong number deserves its own big-number slide with a one-line label.
- Speaker notes carry the explanation the slide leaves out (1–3 sentences).
- Use the audience's vocabulary; define any term a newcomer would not know.
- The closing slide repeats the one sentence you want remembered.
