Company: Tidepool Analytics
One-liner: Water-leak detection for commercial buildings.

## Problem
Undetected leaks cause an average of $250,000 in damage per incident in mid-size office buildings. Facility teams find most leaks only after tenants complain.

## Solution
Clip-on acoustic sensors listen to pipes and flag leaks within 15 minutes. Installation takes under an hour per floor and needs no plumber.

## Market
There are 5.9 million commercial buildings in the US. We estimate the building water-monitoring market at $6.2B.

## Traction
$1.1M ARR from 38 paying customers. Net revenue retention is 120%.

## Business model
Hardware-enabled SaaS: $40 per sensor per month, billed annually.
