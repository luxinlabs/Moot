Company: Kiln Carbon
One-liner: Carbon accounting for ceramics and glass manufacturers.

## Problem
Kiln-heavy manufacturers face new EU carbon border reporting rules, but their emissions data lives in gas meter readings and spreadsheets.

## Solution
Kiln Carbon connects to meters and production logs and produces audit-ready emissions reports per product batch.

## Market
About 18,000 ceramics and glass plants operate in Europe.

## Traction
$640K ARR from 22 plants. Two plants renewed after the first year with 3x expansion.

## Business model
Annual subscription per plant, starting at $24,000.

## Team
Founded in 2024 by a former plant energy manager and a carbon-accounting auditor. Team of 6.

## Ask
Raising a $2.5M seed to cover 3 more countries.
